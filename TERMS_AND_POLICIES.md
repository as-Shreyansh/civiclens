# CIVICLENS — Terms & Conditions and Policies

This document outlines the operational rules, terms of service, and privacy policies governing the **CIVICLENS** civic road safety and defect monitoring platform.

---

## 1. Terms and Conditions

### 1.1 Acceptance of Terms
By accessing or using the CIVICLENS web application, telemetry collection tools, and civic defect reporting interfaces (collectively, the "Platform"), users and contributors agree to adhere to these Terms and Conditions.

### 1.2 Permitted Use & Civic Purpose
CIVICLENS is designed to enable citizens, municipal maintenance teams, and urban infrastructure engineers to log, analyze, and prioritize road hazards (such as potholes, longitudinal cracks, and surface degradation) to improve road safety.

### 1.3 Reporting Integrity & Truthfulness
- All reported road defect observations, descriptions, and uploaded photographs must represent genuine and truthful conditions observed on public roadways.
- The submission of fabricated, deceptive, duplicate, or malicious hazard reports is strictly prohibited.

### 1.4 Road Safety First
- Safety is paramount. Users are strictly prohibited from taking photographs, adjusting sensors, or operating the application while operating a motor vehicle or moving bicycle.
- Users must only document defects when stopped safely off active traffic lanes.

### 1.5 Prototype & Algorithmic Scoring Disclaimer
- The priority score (0–100 scale), severity classification, and anomaly thresholds are automated algorithmic estimates designed for decision support and demonstration.
- These scores do not constitute official civil engineering inspections or guarantees of municipal repair schedules.

### 1.6 User Content & Open Civic Data
- By uploading defect photographs, descriptions, and road notes, users grant CIVICLENS a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to analyze, display, and distribute this data to municipal public works agencies and public dashboards.

### 1.7 Limitation of Liability
- CIVICLENS and its project developers bear no liability for vehicle damage, personal injuries, traffic accidents, or municipal inaction resulting from road conditions, whether logged on the platform or pending repair.

## 2. Privacy Policy

### 2.1 Information Collected
1. **Citizen Defect Reports**: Defect category, description, uploaded surface photos, and timestamp.
2. **Geolocation Data**: Precise GPS latitude and longitude coordinates where defects are identified or telemetry is captured.
3. **Telemetry & Sensor Data**: Accelerometer readings (g-force), speed indicators, and road vibration profiles when telemetry mode is active.
4. **Optional Contact Details**: Email address or contact information provided voluntarily to receive status updates on reported defects.

### 2.2 How Information Is Used
- **Hazard Mapping**: Displaying defects on interactive live maps for citizens and operations dispatch teams.
- **Priority Calculation**: Weighing severity, speed, safety risk, and waiting time to prioritize municipal work orders.
- **Service Verification**: Enabling citizens to track repair progress using unique report tracking IDs.
- **System Improvement**: Refining automated anomaly detection algorithms and aggregate urban road health analytics.

### 2.3 Public Transparency vs. Confidentiality
- **Public Visibility**: Road defect locations, repair statuses, and damage photographs are publicly visible to ensure civic transparency.
- **Private Data Protection**: User contact details, IP addresses, and personal identities are kept confidential and never surfaced on public feeds or dashboards.

### 2.4 Media & Photographic Privacy
- Uploaded defect photos should exclusively capture road surface issues.
- Users should avoid capturing identifiable faces, private residential interiors, or vehicle license plates. Any inadvertent personally identifiable information (PII) may be redacted or blurred.

### 2.5 Data Retention & Security
- Defect and repair history data is retained in a secure persistent database to monitor municipal repair cycles and long-term pavement durability.
- Industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS) and backend access controls are enforced.

### 2.6 No Commercial Sale of Data
- CIVICLENS does not sell, rent, or trade user data or location histories with commercial advertising networks or data brokers.

### 2.7 User Rights
- Users may request corrections, redactions, or removal of submitted reports or associated contact data by providing their unique report tracking ID to the administrative team.
